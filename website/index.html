How to play

Easy to learn, hard to master.

Memorize

Every board starts with a short peek at all tiles. Remember where the symbols are.

Flip two tiles

Tap one tile, then another. Each pair of flips counts as one move.

Make matches

A match stays face up with a ✓. A miss shows ✗ and flips back, so remember it for later.

Earn stars

Clear the board in fewer moves for more stars and a higher score.

FAQ

Is Mind Tiles free?

Yes. The game is free to play and supported by ads. You can optionally watch a short ad to get an extra hint.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All game modes work offline. An internet connection is only used for ads, anonymous analytics and optional notifications.

Do I need to create an account?

No account or sign-in is needed. Your progress is stored on your device.

Will I lose progress if I close the game mid-level?

No. Your board is saved automatically. Tap Continue on the home screen to pick up where you left off.

How do I turn off notifications or analytics?

Open Settings in the game. Daily reminders controls notifications and Share usage & crash reports controls analytics. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset all progress. This permanently erases levels, scores, statistics and achievements on your device.
