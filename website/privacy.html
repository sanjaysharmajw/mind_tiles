In short: Mind Tiles needs no account. Your game progress stays on your device. We use Google AdMob to show ads and Google Firebase to measure gameplay, fix crashes, check performance and, only if you turn it on, send reminders. 1. Who we are

Mind Tiles (the "Game") is published by [DEVELOPER_NAME] ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the processing described in this policy. You can reach us at [CONTACT_EMAIL].

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other directly identifying information, and the Game has no sign-in.

2. Data stored on your device

To save your progress, the Game stores the following only on your device (Android SharedPreferences / iOS UserDefaults). It is not sent to us:

  • settings (sound, music, haptics, theme, preview length, reduced motion, notification and analytics choices)
  • unlocked levels, stars, best scores, times and moves
  • game statistics, Daily Challenge streaks and achievements
  • your current unfinished board, so you can continue it

You can delete this data with Settings → Reset all progress (your settings are kept) or by uninstalling the Game.

3. Data collected by third-party services

The Game includes software development kits (SDKs) from Google LLC. These SDKs collect information directly from your device and process it under Google's own terms.

ServicePurposeData processed
Google AdMob Showing ads (banner, between-game and optional rewarded ads), frequency capping, fraud prevention and measuring ad performance. Advertising ID (Android) or, only if permitted, IDFA (iOS); IP address; device model, OS and language; app version; ad interactions; consent choices.
Firebase Analytics Understanding how the Game is played so we can improve levels and features. App-instance ID; in-game events (for example level started or completed, game mode, difficulty, moves, score, screens viewed); session data; device model, OS version, country/region derived from IP.
Firebase Crashlytics Detecting and fixing crashes and errors. Crash traces, device state at the time of the crash (model, OS, memory, orientation), app-instance and Crashlytics installation IDs.
Firebase Performance Monitoring Measuring start-up time, screen smoothness and level load times. Timing data, device model, OS, network type, app-instance ID.
Firebase Cloud Messaging Sending Daily Challenge reminders and game news, only if you turn on Daily reminders. A messaging token for your device and the topics it is subscribed to.

The analytics events we define do not contain your name, email, contacts, precise location or any free text. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose other than those listed above.

Learn more about how Google uses information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: used by ads and Firebase. Gameplay itself works offline.
  • Notifications: requested only when you switch on Daily reminders. You can decline, and the Game works the same.
  • Advertising ID (Android): used by AdMob. You can reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings.

The Game does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

5. Your choices and controls

  • Analytics, crash reports and performance data: switch off Settings → Share usage & crash reports. Collection by Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring stops immediately.
  • Notifications: switch off Settings → Daily reminders, or turn off notifications for Mind Tiles in your device settings.
  • Ad consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Ad personalization: Android: Settings → Google → Ads (reset or delete your advertising ID). iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete local data: Settings → Reset all progress, or uninstall the Game.

6. Data retention

  • Data on your device is kept until you reset progress or uninstall the Game.
  • Firebase Analytics event data is retained for the period configured in our Google Analytics settings (2 months by default, up to 14 months). Aggregated reports may be kept longer.
  • Crashlytics retains crash stack traces and related data for 90 days.
  • Messaging tokens are deleted by Firebase when they become inactive or when the Game is uninstalled.
  • AdMob data is retained according to Google's policies.

Because we do not hold your name or contact details, we may need the app-instance ID shown in your request to locate data. Write to [CONTACT_EMAIL] and we will respond within the time required by law. You can also manage data held by Google through the links in section 3.

8. Children

Mind Tiles is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). Ads in the Game are restricted to content rated suitable for general audiences ("G"). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.
