## Summary
Mind Tiles needs no account. Your game progress is stored on your device. We use Google services to show ads, to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes.

## Data stored on your device
Settings, unlocked levels, stars, scores, statistics, achievements and your unfinished game are stored only on your device. You can erase them with **Settings → Reset all progress** or by uninstalling the app.

## Data collected by third-party services

| Service (provider) | Purpose | Data | Can I turn it off? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob (Google LLC) | Show ads and measure ad performance | Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions | Consent choices under **Settings → Ad privacy choices** (where required by law). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings. |
| Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) | Understand gameplay (levels played and completed, screens viewed) | App-instance ID, device model, OS version, country, in-game events. No name or email. | **Settings → Share usage & crash reports** |
| Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) | Diagnose crashes | Crash traces, device state, app-instance ID | Same toggle |
| Firebase Performance Monitoring (Google LLC) | Measure app speed | Timing data, device and network type | Same toggle |
| Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC) | Send Daily Challenge reminders | Messaging token | Only if you turn on **Settings → Daily reminders**. Turn it off there or in system settings. |

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, photos or microphone data.
